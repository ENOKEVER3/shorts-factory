# Privacy Policy – Shorts Factory

Shorts Factory is a personal automation tool used only by the owner of the Seunenoch YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel.

- It uses Google sign-in only to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share data from any other person.
- The login key is stored as an encrypted GitHub secret and is never published.
- Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: see the support email on the Google sign-in screen.
